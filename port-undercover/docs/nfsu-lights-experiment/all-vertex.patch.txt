The world's baked shade (2026-11-18): every world vertex shader hands its pixel shader 2 x the vertex's baked light
(max(constant, COLOR.x) doubled; 1 = as lit, below 1 = in shade - a tunnel is mostly that). Below the pivot it is
taken to a power, so shade the game baked gets deeper and places in the open keep their light: core.nfsu
"shade_depth" (launch.c passes RECOMP_NFSU_SHADE_POWER and RECOMP_NFSU_SHADE_PIVOT). Kit: every match replaced.
@@REGEX
(\{ float4 w_ = \(max\(\(c\[\d+\]\.(?:xxxx|wwww)\), \(vv\[1\]\.xxxx\)\)\); r\[0\]\.([xw]) = w_\.\2; \}\n *\{ float4 w_ = \(\(r\[0\]\.\2\2\2\2\) \+ \(r\[0\]\.\2\2\2\2\)\); )([a-z]+\[\d\]\.[xyzw]) = w_\.([xyzw]); \}
@@REPLACE
$1$3 = (w_.$4 >= @ENV(NFSU_SHADE_PIVOT,0.85) ? w_.$4 : @ENV(NFSU_SHADE_PIVOT,0.85) * pow(max(w_.$4, 0.0) / @ENV(NFSU_SHADE_PIVOT,0.85), @ENV(NFSU_SHADE_POWER,1.0))); }
@@END
